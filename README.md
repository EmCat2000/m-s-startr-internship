# Sage.Education Starter

Welcome. This is your starter repo for the Sage.Education™ Junior Internship. Think of it as a clean desk to practise on: you'll copy it to your own account, save a few versions, and try the time-travel moves from the guide, all in your browser.

You don't need to install anything, and you can't break this. If something ever looks off, you can step back to an earlier version. That's the whole point of version control.

## How to use it

1. Click the green **Use this template** button near the top of this page, then **Create a new repository**. That makes your own copy under your account.
2. Give it a name (your first name plus "pitfall-filter" works well), keep it **Public**, and click **Create**.
3. Open `my-pitfall-filter.md`, click the pencil to edit, and follow the guide: save a version, look back through the history, make a branch, and open a pull request.

The full step-by-step is in the guide chapter **Time travel with version control**: <https://sage.education/how-to-build-an-ai/time-travel-with-version-control/>.

## What's in here

`my-pitfall-filter.md` is a short starter list of planning traps. Add your own trap, in your own words. It's the same list you build in the Week 5 notebook, so it's a head start on next week's first contribution.

## A note on the master branch

You'll notice your main line of work here is called **master**, not "main". That's on purpose, and it's the convention we keep across our projects.

The word comes from crafts that have used it a long time. When a record or a CD is pressed, every copy comes from the master. A goldsmith casts from a master mould. Each time, the master is the trusted original that every good copy comes from. That's what this branch is for your work.

The buttons and steps work the same whether a project calls its main line "master" or "main", so nothing here locks you in. We just like naming it after the crafts that got there first.

## Whose work is this?

Yours. Anything you make in your copy belongs to you. If you later choose to share a piece of it back to one of our projects, we'll walk you through that together, and it stays your call.
